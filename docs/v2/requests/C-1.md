# C-1: paper InkDoc conventions lane B's core can rely on, and a params mirror ask

Lane C · See. Status: open (owner to record the accepted items in `docs/v2/CONTRACTS.md`).

## 1. Features follow core/ink/features.ts exactly

`vision/app/cv/features.py` is a line-by-line Python port of `computeGeo`, `featuresOf` and `computeComponents`
(same constants, JS `Math.round` rounding). Paper components carry `f`, `bbox` (q16, ink extents) and `area`
(vector ink area, 3 decimals) computed by that port from the vectorized strokes.

- **Ask.** When `features.ts` changes, either tell lane C, or (simpler, recommended) have the worker recompute `f`
  for every component with `featuresOf(computeGeo(strokes))` whatever the source, so the definitions live in one place.
- **What differs from the canvas is only the grouping.** A paper component is one connected blob of one classified
  ink colour (the canvas unions strokes that touch, gap ≤ 0.5·w). The results agree for touching strokes. A pole
  standing on a floor is one component with two strokes (floor, pole) on both paths.

## 2. Stroke conventions on paper

- **Stroke decomposition.** Strokes come from a skeleton graph split at junctions by straightest continuation
  (turn < 35°). A floor stays one stroke, and a pole standing on it ends at the floor's centreline.
- **Stroke kinds.**
  - `dot`: taps and marker dots, including round filled marks up to 1.3 cells. `w = [diameter]`.
  - `blob`: filled shapes. `pts` is the outline (approxPolyDP 0.08 cell), `w = [median pen width]`.
  - `line`: everything else. One width per point.
- **Ids.** Strokes are `s1..sN` and components `1..M`, both in route order: left to right in 2-cell columns, then top
  to bottom. Paper `sheet` is the 0-based sheet index. Raster ingest sets `sheet` to the screen index, as the canvas
  does.
- **Rules box (paper sheet 1).** Its strokes are in `InkDoc.strokes` with ids `r1..rK`, listed in `rules_ink`, and
  they are **not** in any component.
  - Their coordinates are drawing-area cells, so **y > 27** (the box sits below the drawing area).
  - `perceiveHeuristic`, fit and compile must skip every id in `rules_ink`.
  - PERCEIVE reads the handwriting from the annotated sheet image.
- **Character box (sheet 1).** `character.strokes` (`c1..`) are in cells from the box interior's top-left
  (0..≈6.2 per side, 5 mm cells), with `sheet` 0.
- **Noise.** `noise.count` counts marks under 0.03 cell² that were not vectorized. Pencil marks (the gray class) are
  not ink. They raise a `warning.pencil_marks` instead.

## 4. Deviations from PLAN_V2 §3.2 (product reasons)

- **Taps up to 1.3 cells become dots.** The plan's dot rule (max side ≤ 0.9 cell, fill ≥ 0.5) misses 20 px taps,
  which are 1.25 cells. Round filled marks up to 1.3 cells (fill ≥ 0.6, circ ≥ 0.7, no hole) are dots too.
- **A dot needs bbox aspect ≤ 2.** Otherwise a short straight dash, which fills its bbox, becomes a dot.
- **Spur pruning keeps real short strokes.** A spur shorter than max(0.4 cell, 1.5·w) is kept when it protrudes
  ≥ 0.25 cell beyond the host stroke's edge (arrow barbs, letter strokes).
- **Mixed components are split.** A mostly thin component with a filled core ≥ 1 cell² becomes a blob plus lines,
  instead of all-blob or all-skeleton.
- **Rectification uses every marker corner.** All corners of the detected markers feed one RANSAC homography, so
  3 markers give a true perspective fit. v1 completed a parallelogram, which is affine-only and drifts under tilt.
