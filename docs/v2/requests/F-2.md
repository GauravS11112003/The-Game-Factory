(§4.2 "node keys (for the reach halo)"). The key format is not specified.

**What lane F parses** (`web/src/app/core_tasks.ts` `reachCells`), in order:
1. `"x,y"` or `"[x,y]"` in cells (feet position), optionally followed by `|…` (bitmask, phase);
2. `"<surface object id>:<x>"` (the stub's form, e.g. `"o3:40.00"`), optionally followed by `:…`; y is read from that
   object's fitted surface (`objects[].shape.segs`), else from the compiled tiles in that column.
3. If nothing parses, it falls back to `route[].from/to` and `frontier.reached`.

**Ask.** Emit one of the two forms above (x in cells, not px), deduplicated per 4 px. Form 1 is preferred because it
needs no geometry lookup.
