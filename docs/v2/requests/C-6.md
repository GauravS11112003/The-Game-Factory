### `SheetInfo.x_inherited`

On a paper remix, every screen that was not scanned keeps the parent's ink. Its `SheetInfo` is `{index, x_inherited:
true}`, with no photo and no QR. Please record this extension, or promote it to a field (`inherited: bool = False`).

## 2. Paper remix InkDocs now carry the parent's strokes

A paper remix or extension InkDoc now has:
- every parent stroke, with origin `parent`, ids `p1..`, and the parent's own components (rules-box strokes left out);
- the kid's new marker ink, with origin `kid`, ids `s1..`;
- `ink.parent` set.

This matches the canvas remix (`web/src/app/screens/draw.ts`). It already makes N10 dedupe keep the parent's coins,
and an untouched remix sheet adds no object.

**Ask.** `remixDiff` (`core/scene/remix.ts`) is still a `NotImplemented` stub. It is what matches a kid's *retrace* of
a parent object (IoU ≥ 0.3, or centre within 1 cell) to that object, so the retrace keeps the parent's id and does not
become a second ground line. It is also what lets lane D skip PERCEIVE on inherited screens, because the parent's
roles and bindings carry over (C-5 §4).

## 3. N11 `join_seam` and touching ends

`normalize.ts` skips every pair with `gap <= 0`. On paper, a ground line drawn to the very edge of sheet 1 and on from
the very edge of sheet 2 often touches or overlaps by a hair. Those pairs get no join. A Δy of up to 1 cell then stays
as a 1-row step at the seam.

`ScanV2.seams` lists these pairs, with their averaged seam point. Please allow `gap ≤ 0` at a paper seam, for example
by snapping both ends to the averaged point as §6.4 describes.

**Extend sheets.** The parent ends at world x = 48·k_parent, and the extend sheet's ink starts 2 columns before it.
`Math.round(x1 / 48) * 48` already finds that seam.

## 4. Stroke `sheet` on paper

`InkStroke.sheet` is the 0-based world screen the photo was placed at. On an extend sheet, that includes the 2
overlap columns left of the seam. Parent strokes carry `floor(x / 48)`.
